4. Collect

Show your OTP to the guard and collect it.

Know how this operates

CampusParcel is a web based application which you can use to collect your parcel in campus securely, without any hassle and without any chance of missing it.

If you book a parcel on COD you can share the OTP with the guards beforehand, or during the delivery, just by filling a small form on the "Register Your Parcel" page. Even if you are getting a parcel from a friend or family member, you can register it on that page too.

Guards receive and hand over parcels using their own separate Guard Panel (PIN protected). If you are a student or faculty member you can go to the "My Parcels" page to check or collect your parcel.

For any help, support or complaints you can go to the "Help & Support" section.
