Know how this operates

Campus Parcel is a Web based application which you can use to collect your parcel in campus securely without any hassle and chances of missing them.


If you are a guard you can recive the parcels securely by going to Guards page (In the Navigation Pannel) and if you are a student/faculty you can go to the My Parcel page to check or collect you parcel (In the Navigation Pannel)


For any help or Support you can go to Help and support section.
